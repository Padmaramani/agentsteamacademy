Padma Ramani is a seasoned computer science engineer with 16 years of global industry experience at leading organizations, including Experian, Kaiser Permanente, and IBM. Her journey into teaching began in the summer of 2015, when she introduced Scratch programming to her daughter and neighborhood children—a discovery that ignited her passion for education. Over the past decade, Padma has taught programming skills to K-12 students and adults alike, impacting the lives of more than 500 learners. Under her guidance, many high school and middle school students have successfully passed the PCEP exam. Today, Padma continues to innovate in computer science education, collaborating with industry experts to expand and enrich her curriculum.
